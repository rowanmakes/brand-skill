STRONGEST CONCEPT AND WHY:
**Concept A (腰线半山)** is the strongest strategic direction because it is the only concept that honours the client's "中文为主，英文为辅" requirement and builds a memorable, one-sentence visual idea directly from the brand name「半山」(extending the mid-stroke of「半」as a mid-mountain horizon cut across「山」) without resorting to generic coffee-bean clip-art or outdoor camping badges like competitor AOKKA. Its main risk is **execution craft**: right now the 45° arrow/picket spikes make the mark look rigid and aggressive, the `20`-unit counters of「山」and `8`-unit arrow gap clog at 16 px, and the lockup abandons the mark entirely in favour of a Songti wordmark with a printer-glitch horizontal slit across「山」. Refining the「半山」monogram (removing the spikes, opening the cut and counters to `≥24` units, harmonising stroke weights with a warm, restrained touch) and pairing it properly with an intact「半山烘焙 · BANSHAN ROASTERS」lockup will make this a distinctive, ownable identity that works from a 16 px browser favicon to a single-colour thermal batch label.
