STRONGEST CONCEPT AND WHY:
**Concept A (腰线半山)** is clearly the strongest concept to take forward. The Round 2 revision (`concept-a-v3.svg`) resolved every geometric and scaling flaw from Round 1: removing the aggressive 45° arrow/picket spikes and opening the mid-slope horizon cut (`24` units), inter-character gap (`24` units), and internal counters of「山」(`25` units) gives the mark a calm, transparent, architectural authority ("克制、通透、精准") that remains legible down to 16 px and prints cleanly on single-colour thermal labels. Above all, it is the only direction that turns the Chinese brand name「半山」itself into an unmistakable visual signature without relying on a Western initial (`B`) or pictorial mountain clip-art (`C`). Its main remaining risk is the **1:2 character-width jump between `半山` and `烘焙` in `concept-a-lockup-v3.svg`**, which is easily solved in final refinement by either widening `半` and `山` in the horizontal 4-character wordmark or pairing the square `半山` mark with a uniform-width `半山烘焙` wordmark.
